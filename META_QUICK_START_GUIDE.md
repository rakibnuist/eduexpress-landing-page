## 📊 Week 1 Monitoring Checklist

### Daily Checks (Days 1-7)

**Morning (9-10 AM):**
- [ ] Check total spend (should be ~₹1,000/day)
- [ ] Check leads generated (in form or Meta Lead Form)
- [ ] Calculate current CPL (Spend ÷ Leads)
- [ ] Review lead quality (call/WhatsApp top 3-5 leads)

**Evening (7-8 PM):**
- [ ] Check CTR (should be >1.5%)
- [ ] Check frequency (should be <2 first week)
- [ ] Review comments on ads (respond within 1 hour)
- [ ] Pause any ad with <0.5% CTR after ₹500 spend

### Actions Based on Results

**If CPL > ₹500:**
- ❌ Pause ad immediately
- 🔍 Check: Is targeting too broad? Is creative unclear?

**If CTR < 1%:**
- ⚠️ Creative not resonating
- 🎨 Try different image or ad copy

**If Frequency > 3:**
- 🔄 Ad fatigue setting in
- 💡 Expand audience or refresh creative

**If Lead Quality < 50%:**
- 🎯 Targeting too broad
- ✏️ Add clearer eligibility criteria in ad copy
- 📝 Add qualification questions to form

## 🔧 Quick Troubleshooting

### "My ad was rejected"
**Common reasons:**
- Too much text in image (>20%)
- Misleading claims
- Poor landing page experience

**Fix:** Reduce image text, ensure claims are accurate, optimize page speed

### "High cost per click but no conversions"
**Problem:** Landing page issue

**Fix:** 
- Check mobile experience
- Ensure form is working
- Verify Pixel is firing CompleteRegistration event

### "Low CTR (<1%)"
**Problem:** Ad creative not engaging

**Fix:**
- Use more emotional/urgent copy
- Test different images
- Try video instead of static image

### "Getting leads but all are spam/fake"
**Problem:** Targeting too broad or form too easy

**Fix:**
- Narrow age range (e.g., 19-24 instead of 18-25)
- Add phone number validation
- Include GPA/qualification question
