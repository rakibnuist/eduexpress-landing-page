# 🚀 EduExpress International: Complete Meta Marketing Guideline

This guide is designed to help you launch high-performing Facebook and Instagram ads for your Study Abroad agency. We have already set up the "Technical Foundation" (Pixel & Events) on your landing page. Now, let's build the marketing machine.

---

## ✅ Phase 1: Technical Checklist (Before You Spend $1)

Ensure these are green before launching:
1.  **Pixel Active:** Verify `ViewContent` and `CompleteRegistration` are firing using the "Meta Pixel Helper" chrome extension.
2.  **Domain Verified:** Your domain (`eduexpressint.com`) must be verified in Facebook Business Manager.
3.  **Aggregated Event Measurement:** Prioritize `CompleteRegistration` as your #1 event in Events Manager (critical for iOS14+ tracking).

---

## 🎯 Phase 2: Campaign Strategy (The "2-Step Funnel")

Since you are a new brand without a huge reputation yet, we will use a **Filter & Close** strategy.

### Campaign A: The "Hook" (Cold Audience)
*   **Goal:** Get cheap traffic to the landing page and let the "No IELTS" hook filter them.
*   **Objective:** **Traffic** (Optimization: Landing Page Views) OR **Leads** (Optimization: Performance goals). *Recommended: Leads (Website Conversion).*
*   **Budget:** 60% of total budget.

### Campaign B: The "Reminder" (Retargeting)
*   **Goal:** Convert people who visited but didn't apply.
*   **Objective:** **Sales / Leads** (Optimization: `CompleteRegistration` Event).
*   **Audience:** Custom Audience (Website Visitors - Last 30 Days).
*   **Budget:** 40% of total budget.

---

## 🛠 Phase 3: Campaign Setup Guide

### 1. Daily Budget Planning
*   **Start Small:** $10 - $20 USD / day.
*   **Split:** $12 for Cold Audience, $8 for Retargeting.
*   **Rule:** Don't touch a running ad for 3 days. Let the AI learn.

### 2. Audience Targeting (Who to show ads to?)

**Set A: The "Dreamers" (Broad Interest)**
*   **Location:** Bangladesh (Exclude extremely rural areas if you want higher paying capacity).
*   **Age:** 18 - 28 (Core student demographic).
*   **Detailed Targeting:**
    *   *Interests:* Study abroad, IELTS, Student visa, Higher education, British Council, IDP Education.
    *   *Universities:* Dhaka University, North South University, BRAC University (Good for solvency).

**Set B: The "Desperate" (Specific Problems)**
*   **Age:** 20 - 30.
*   **Interests:** Study abroad, IELTS, Student visa, Higher education, CSC Scholarship, Study in China.

## 📊 Phase 5: Optimization (How to Win)

Check your Ads Manager every morning.

| Metric | Benchmark | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CTR (Link Click-Through)** | < 1.0% | **Bad.** Change your Ad Image immediately. Stop the ad. |
| **CTR** | > 1.5% | **Good.** Keep running. |
| **CPC (Cost Per Click)** | > $0.10 | **Expensive.** Your audience is too small or bored. broaden targeting. |
| **Landing Page Views** | Low | If Clicks are high but Views are low, your site is slow. (We fixed this with Vercel/Next.js). |
| **Results (Registrations)** | < $2.00 | **Excellent.** Scale budget by 20%. |
